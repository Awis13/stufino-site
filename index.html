Stufino

A simple, fast app for pellet stoves with a Duepi EVO control board. Turn the stove on and off, set the power and the temperature, watch it burn, and see charts of the last days. iPhone, iPad and Mac, with widgets, Control Center, Live Activity, Siri and Shortcuts.

  • Set up your stove
  • The Stufino Recorder (optional, for history around the clock)
  • Privacy
  • Support

No stove at hand? The app has a demo stove that runs on your device.

Stufino is an independent app. It is not made or endorsed by the maker of any stove or control board.
