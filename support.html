Common questions

The app says "No answer". The relay allows one client per stove. Close the vendor's app and try again, or use the Recorder.

Where is my device code? See Setup.

Siri does not start a command. On iOS 27 the app's built-in phrases may only open the app. Create a Shortcut: Shortcuts, Add Action, Stufino, Turn On Stove, and name it "Turn on the stove".
