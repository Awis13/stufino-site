Set up your stove

1. Have your device code or bridge address ready

The code is printed on the stove's Wi-Fi module (the small board or dongle fitted to the stove).

2. Choose how the app reaches the stove

  • Local: a serial-to-Wi-Fi bridge on the stove's board (for example ESP-Link). Nothing leaves your home.
  • Cloud: a relay server and your device code. The app has no server built in; you enter the address yourself.
  • Auto: tries Local first, then Cloud.

3. The server address

The relay address is the one the vendor's own app connects to: duepiwebserver2.com, port 3000 (the port is under Advanced in the app).

The relay serves one client per stove. If the vendor's app connects at the same time, one of the two is disconnected. The Recorder avoids this.

4. Test and save

Tap Test connection, then Save. Turning the stove on or off always asks for confirmation.

Safety

Never leave a burning stove unattended or rely on the app alone. Readings can be delayed or out of date, and a command can fail or not arrive. Reset an alarm only after you have inspected the stove and found the cause. Keep working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms near the stove.
