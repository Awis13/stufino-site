Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Stufino does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting. The developer receives nothing from your device.

What the app sends, and where

The app sends the stove's device code and your commands only to the destination you choose:

  • the stove's Wi-Fi module on your home network (Local),
  • the relay server whose address you enter (Cloud),
  • or your own Stufino Recorder (through an end-to-end encrypted tunnel).

Those destinations are run by you or by third parties, and their own policies apply to them.

What stays on your device

Your stoves, settings and the history the app records are stored on your device and in the app's shared storage for widgets. Deleting the app deletes them.
