The Stufino Recorder

An optional small program that you run at home on a computer that is always on (a NAS, a Raspberry Pi, a home server). It watches the stove around the clock and gives the app:

  • History around the clock: charts for days and months, not only while the app is open.
  • Control from anywhere without opening ports or creating accounts: the app reaches the recorder through an end-to-end encrypted tunnel.
  • No fighting over the vendor cloud: the recorder holds the one allowed connection and the app goes through it, so commands are never duplicated.

Status

The Recorder is not published yet. The app works without it: Local and Cloud connections need nothing else.

Pairing

The recorder prints a pairing code and a QR code. In the app: Settings, your stove, Recorder, Scan pairing code. Anyone who has the code can watch and control the stove, so keep it private.

Stufino is an independent app. It is not made or endorsed by the maker of any stove or control board.
